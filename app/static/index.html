Is your board RoHS, REACH and export compliant?

Drop the bill of materials (BOM) exported from your CAD tool or ERP. Every component is matched against a reference database and gets its RoHS 2, RoHS 3 and REACH status, the RoHS exemptions it relies on, and its ECCN export classification. Problem parts are flagged, with an equivalent from another manufacturer when there is one.

What it checks

RoHS 2

Directive 2011/65/EU: lead, mercury, cadmium, hexavalent chromium, PBB and PBDE in electrical and electronic equipment.

RoHS 3

Delegated Directive (EU) 2015/863: adds four phthalates (DEHP, BBP, DBP, DIBP), ten restricted substances in total.

RoHS exemptions

Exemptions of Annexes III and IV used by each part, with the official EUR-Lex text and the expiry dates to anticipate.

REACH

Regulation (EC) 1907/2006: substances of very high concern (SVHC) above 0.1 % by weight, and Annex XVII restrictions.

Export control (ECCN)

Classification on the US Commerce Control List (EAR99, 3A991…), explained field by field, with the official definition.

How it works

  1. Drop your BOM

    CSV, TXT or Excel file from KiCad, Altium, an ERP… Columns are recognised automatically; for an unusual format, map them yourself with a live preview.

  2. Matching

    Each line is matched by manufacturer part number, with manufacturer names normalised (TI = Texas Instruments) and close or ambiguous matches flagged.

  3. Verdicts

    A status per regulation and an overall verdict per line and for the whole board: compliant, exemption, SVHC, non-compliant or needs review, with the reason.

  4. Act

    Replace a problem part with an equivalent from another manufacturer, then export the Excel report or the modified BOM.

Who it is for

Hardware designers

Check the parts while designing, before the prototype, and pick compliant alternatives early.

Purchasing and component engineering

Find equivalents from other manufacturers and spot the parts whose exemption is about to expire.

Quality and compliance

Export control

See at a glance which parts carry an ECCN other than EAR99 and may need an export licence.
